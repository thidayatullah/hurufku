Hurufku

A shared canvas for preschool reading. Draw letters or scribbles, arrange them into words, and hear them read aloud — right in the browser, no login required.

Open the canvas →

How it works

  1. 1

    Draw or type

    Scribble freely with a marker (finger, mouse, or stylus), or use the keyboard to drop letter stickers straight onto the board.

  2. 2

    Watch scribbles become stickers

    After a short pause, strokes drawn close together turn into one movable sticker you can drag, resize, and recolor.

  3. 3

    Arrange into words

    Line letter stickers up left-to-right to spell simple words like BA, BABA, or ABA.

  4. 4

    Hear it read aloud

    Select letters and press Speak to hear them read in English or Indonesian.

  5. 5

    Pick up where you left off

    Hurufku remembers your last board on this device — no account, no cloud sync.

Works everywhere

Hurufku runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets, Chromebooks, and PCs. Open the canvas once, and it keeps working offline afterward — install it to your home screen for quick access.
